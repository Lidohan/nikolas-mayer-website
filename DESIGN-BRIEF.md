# Design brief — how to give design feedback

The fastest way to improve the design is to provide 2–5 references.

You can send:
- links to personal/professional websites you like;
- screenshots of individual sections;
- screenshots from unrelated websites where you like the typography, navigation, colour system or layout;
- a rough drawing of the homepage;
- one sentence such as “more ECB / less influencer” or “more magazine / less corporate”.

For each reference, say what you want to copy conceptually, for example:
- typography;
- amount of whitespace;
- portrait treatment;
- navigation;
- cards / timeline;
- dark vs light;
- institutional vs personal tone;
- how videos are presented.

## Three useful directions for this project

### A. Institutional editorial — current starter
Warm off-white background, restrained navy, serif display typography, strong biography and source-of-record feel. Best if the main objective is credibility and authority.

### B. Swiss / research minimal
White/near-white, black typography, grid-heavy layout, very little decoration, dates and institutions prominent. Best if you want an LSE/ECB/researcher feel.

### C. Markets / data
Dark background, sharper information hierarchy, compact data-led modules and visual references to financial terminals/research dashboards. Best if you want the site to feel more like a market economist / analyst publication.

The content architecture can stay the same while the visual layer changes completely.
