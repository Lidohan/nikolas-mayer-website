About

Economic analysis across institutions, markets and policy.

Nikolas Mayer is a Czech economist whose career has spanned central banking, financial-market analysis, banking supervision, macroeconomic surveillance and European policy.

Background

He studied Government and Economics at the London School of Economics and Quantitative Economics and Finance at the University of St. Gallen.

His professional experience includes the European Central Bank, European Commission, European Parliament and European Systemic Risk Board Secretariat.

The purpose of this site is to make professional information and primary sources easy to verify, rather than to replace institutional records.
