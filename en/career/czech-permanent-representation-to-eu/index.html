Assistant — finance and trade policy

Institution
Permanent Representation of the Czech Republic to the EU
Role
Assistant — finance and trade policy
Period
1. 9. 2011 – 14. 10. 2011
Location
Brussels, Belgium

At the Permanent Representation of the Czech Republic to the European Union in Brussels I gained my first practical experience with EU legislation and Council negotiations. In the finance and trade-policy area I followed negotiations on post-financial-crisis financial regulation, analysed proposals from the perspective of Czech interests and prepared debriefing for the Czech Ministry of Finance. Documented files included the emerging CRD IV/CRR package implementing Basel III, the proposal on OTC derivatives, central counterparties and trade repositories that later became EMIR, and the proposed EU regulation on short selling and credit default swaps. The role combined legislative reading, participation in meetings, assessment of implications for the Czech financial sector and communication between Brussels and the national administration.
