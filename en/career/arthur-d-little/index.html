At Arthur D. Little in Prague I worked as a research analyst on a strategy engagement for a large Eastern European utility. I performed statistical analysis of financial data and company assets as input into a capital-investment strategy. A second workstream assessed opportunities for expansion into new business sectors. The project required structuring financial information, comparing investment options and translating data into decision material for the client's broader strategy. The engagement was confidential, so I do not publicly identify the client.
