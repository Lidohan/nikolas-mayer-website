At Boston Consulting Group in Prague I worked as a Visiting Associate on a strategy engagement for the launch of a new mobile-network operator. My work focused on quantitative analysis of the competitive market environment, market structure, pricing levels and development of the prospective operator's pricing strategy. In addition to analytical work, I had responsibilities directly within the client team, combining desk research and data analysis with translation of the findings into concrete strategic decisions. The role was short but important as early intensive experience in commercial strategy before my subsequent focus on European economic institutions.
