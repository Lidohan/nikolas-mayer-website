Location
St. Gallen, Switzerland

The M.A. HSG in Quantitative Economics and Finance at the University of St. Gallen combined advanced macroeconomics, microeconomics, mathematics, statistics, Econometrics I and II, microeconometrics, dynamic programming, asset pricing and theory of finance. My master's thesis focused on GDP forecasting for European economies and compared Bayesian VAR models with neural networks using Bayesian regularisation. The research involved optimisation of parameters, variables and model specifications and comparison of forecast performance. The programme provided the quantitative foundation for later econometric and forecasting work at DG ECFIN, the ECB and ESRB. A public graduate list from the 8 October 2016 Master Graduation Day explicitly lists “Mayer — Nikolas — Moravany — Tschechische Republik” among the thirteen graduates of the programme.
