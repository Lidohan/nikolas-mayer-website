About

Nikolas Mayer is a Czech economist with experience at the European Parliament, European Commission and European Central Bank.

Professional background

He has worked at the European Central Bank, European Commission and European Parliament. At the ECB his work covered financial stability, macroprudential policy, bond and foreign-exchange markets and supervisory banking statistics. At DG ECFIN he worked on macroeconomic forecasts, economic surveillance and the Recovery and Resilience Facility. At the European Parliament he works on economic, financial and budgetary legislation.

The purpose of the site is to make professional information, publications and primary sources easy to verify while building a permanent archive of public economic analysis.
