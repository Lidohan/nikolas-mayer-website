About

Nikolas Mayer

I am a Czech economist and macroeconomic forecaster with experience at the European Central Bank, European Commission and European Parliament. I spent seven years in the central-banking and financial-stability environment; at the European Commission I prepared macroeconomic forecasts and analysis of Member State economies. I now use that experience in my own data-driven analysis of the Czech economy and its performance in European comparison.
