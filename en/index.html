Nikolas Mayer

Economist

I work at the European Parliament and previously worked at the European Commission and European Central Bank.

My work covers economic and financial policy, macroeconomic forecasting, financial markets, banking and European legislation.

Selected experience

2025–present

European Parliament

Administrator advising on parliamentary business

Economic, financial and budgetary work across ECON, BUDG and FISC, including financial legislation, capital markets, the EU budget, MFF and RRF.

2022–2025

European Commission

Economic Analyst, DG ECFIN

Macroeconomic forecasts, the European Semester, MIP, fiscal analysis and the Recovery and Resilience Facility.

2015–2022

European Central Bank

Financial stability, markets and banking data

Macroprudential Policy, Bond and International Markets, Supervisory Banking Statistics and the ESRB Secretariat.
