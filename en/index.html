Economics, markets and European policy.

Nikolas Mayer is a Czech economist with professional experience at the European Parliament, European Commission and European Central Bank.

His work spans EU financial legislation and budgets, macroeconomic forecasting and surveillance, financial stability, banking data, bond and foreign-exchange markets and quantitative economic analysis.
